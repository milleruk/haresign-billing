The subscription

Which plan an organisation is on, whether it is active, and when it next renews or ends. Kept against the organisation, not against the person who happened to pay.

What it unlocks

The products a plan includes, and the ones it does not. A PCN’s subscription can cover its member practices, and each practice can see that it does without seeing what the PCN pays.

Contacts and invoices

Who to send a billing question to, and links to the invoices themselves. Card details are never entered or stored here — payment happens on our payment provider’s own pages.
